# 1,000+ dataset robustness report — 2026-09-25

## Method

Ran the app's native on-device OCR on the supplied `RecallLater_1000plus_realistic_dataset.zip` archive. The archive contains 1,000 JPG captures, 20 companion PDFs, and labels for 100 underlying scenarios with 10 image variants each. All **1,020 files produced OCR text or barcode data**.

Scored the deterministic rules parser against the dataset's expected fields. No model requests or AI-only results were included. Training and validation examples were used to guide parser fixes; the 150-image test split and its three PDFs were left report-only. Image variants share the same semantic parent, so file-level scores are robustness measurements rather than 1,000 independent examples.

## Robustness across parents and variants

- The 100 unaugmented base images scored **80/100 exact**.
- **64/100** parent scenarios were exact on every image variant; **80/100** were exact on at least half their variants.
- Exact image scores by variant ranged from **76/100** for JPEG-share compression to **80/100** for blur, edge crop, and warm cast.

All six validation movie/show scenarios expect the platform `Streambox`, but that value is absent from their OCR and barcode data. The parser extracts the type and title but leaves the platform blank rather than guessing. These six scenarios account for the validation exact-score shortfall. Test-set failures also include categories reserved for that split; they remain diagnostic and were not used to tune rules.

## Changes exercised

The larger set helped verify and improve OCR handling for event dates with joined weekdays and zero-for-O month names, restaurant posts with social-app context, order-product titles, currency prefixes, and coupon discount labels. Regression tests, lint, and TypeScript checks passed.
