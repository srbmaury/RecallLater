# Play Store listing (draft)

**App name:** RecallLater: share it, remember it

**Short description (80 max):**
Share a bill, ticket or message. RecallLater reminds you when it matters.

**Full description:**

Screenshots of bills, tickets and coupons pile up and get forgotten. RecallLater turns them
into reminders.

Share anything to RecallLater: a screenshot, a PDF, a WhatsApp message, a shopping link. It
reads it on your phone, finds what matters, and brings it back at the right time.

• Bills: the amount and due date, with a reminder that repeats until you mark it paid
• Tickets: flight, train and bus details, and a nudge before you leave
• Coupons: the code, one tap to copy, a reminder before it expires
• Messages: "Can you send the deck by Friday?" becomes a to-do with a deadline
• Receipts: return-by dates and warranties you'd otherwise lose track of
• Also: jobs to apply for, places to try, books, shows, recipes and Wi-Fi QR codes

Private by design
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking
• Text is read on your phone, even offline
• AI help is optional, and only ever sees text with personal numbers removed
• App lock, and passphrase-protected backups

Made for India
• Understands ₹ amounts, Indian bills and UPI QR codes
• Reads Hindi and mixed Hindi–English bills

**Category:** Productivity

**Data safety answers:** No data collected. No data shared. (Optional AI: text is processed
ephemerally and not stored; declare it as "processed ephemerally" if the AI server ships.)
